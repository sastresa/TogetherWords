# English (United States) Play listing

**App name** (30 characters maximum): Words & Pause

**Short description** (80 characters maximum): Help children express needs and take a gentle pause during tantrums

**Full description** (4,000 characters maximum):

Words & Pause is a quiet app for a young child and an adult to use together. It helps children practise expressing what they need while calm and offers a simple choice when a tantrum is beginning.

Practise my words: Tap a large card to hear “No,” “Stop,” “Help,” or “I need a break.” Children can listen, say the words too, or practise with toys. There is no microphone, recording, or speech evaluation.

I need help now: Choose words to say something, or tap a gentle balloon that grows and rests while you pause together. There is no timer, score, or required rhythm. The child can leave or change activities at any time.

For adults: Read practical guidance about why tantrums happen and ways to support a child before, during, and after one. The app is designed to be used with an adult alongside the child.

The full experience is available in English and Spanish. The app follows the device language where supported, and adults can switch languages in the app.

Words & Pause does not promise to prevent tantrums or replace adult support. It has no ads, accounts, or analytics.

**Release notes** (first release): First release with English and Spanish phrase cards, a gentle balloon activity, and guidance for adults.
